About me

From chemical engineering to agentic AI

I'm Crista Villatoro-Müller, a Data & AI Consultant based in Berlin. My path started in chemical and environmental engineering — a background that taught me to break complex systems into measurable, solvable parts. That same mindset now drives how I build data and AI solutions.

Today I support Airbus and CIMPA (a Sopra Steria company), where I own a data governance portfolio of 40+ initiatives — designing data products, running data quality campaigns, and building the monitoring dashboards teams rely on across enterprise data platforms like Palantir Skywise.

As an officially nominated AI Accelerator, I'm the hands-on builder and go-to person for AI use cases: developing prototypes, prompts, automations, and multi-agent systems, while coaching colleagues and driving practical AI adoption across the organization.

What I enjoy most is turning an idea into a working demo — fast. Proof-of-concepts that make AI tangible for real business problems.
